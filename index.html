Myo

A personal iPhone app that turns a Fitbit's data into daily Sleep, Recovery, Strain and Stress scores, alongside a strength-training log.

Myo reads sleep, heart rate, heart-rate variability, blood oxygen, skin temperature, steps and workouts from Google Health with your permission. It works the scores out on your phone, and each score shows the readings behind it.

Myo is built and used by one person. It isn't on the App Store and it isn't a medical device.
