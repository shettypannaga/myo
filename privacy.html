Privacy policy

Last updated 20 October 2026.

What Myo reads from Google

When you connect Google Health, Myo asks for read-only access to your sleep, activity and fitness, and health metrics: sleep stages, heart rate, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, blood oxygen, skin temperature, breathing rate, steps, weight and exercise sessions. Myo never writes to or deletes anything in your Google account.

Where it's kept

This data is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage, and is used only to calculate and show your scores. It isn't sent to Myo's developer or to any server, and it isn't shared, sold or used for advertising. Your Google sign-in is kept in the iPhone's Keychain.

Optional backup

If you choose to sign in to Myo's own account, the workouts and routines you log in Myo, and the body details you enter (birth date, sex, height and weight), are backed up so they survive reinstalling the app. Google Health data isn't part of this backup.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Myo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Disconnect Google Health in Myo's Settings to stop further access, or remove Myo's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the phone.
